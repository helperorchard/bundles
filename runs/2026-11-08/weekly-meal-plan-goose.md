## Content

# Weekly Meal Plan

Use this on the weekend to plan the week's dinners.

## Inputs
- Number of people, diet needs, and dislikes (from memory).
- What is already in the fridge and pantry.
- Busy nights when cooking must take under 20 minutes.

## Steps
1. Plan 5 dinners (or the number asked). Use up the fridge items that spoil soonest in the first two nights.
2. Put quick meals on the busy nights.
3. Reuse ingredients across meals so half a bunch of something is not wasted, and say where.
4. For each dinner give: name, rough time, and the main ingredients. Not full recipes unless asked.
5. Build one shopping list grouped by store section, leaving out what the user already has.

## Rules
- Respect every allergy in memory; when unsure if an item is safe, leave it out and say why.
- Do not order groceries or fill any cart.

## Done when
The dinner plan and the grouped shopping list are returned.

## Weekly Dinner Plan (5 nights, for 2 people)

**Diet constraints applied:** vegetarian • peanut-free (no peanuts) • no mushrooms

### Dinners

1. **Monday — Sweet Potato & Spinach Chickpea Curry over Rice** *(~35 min)*
   - **Main ingredients:** sweet potatoes, spinach, canned chickpeas, rice, coconut milk + curry paste.
   - **Fridge used:** sweet potatoes, spinach, chickpeas, rice.

2. **Tuesday — Spinach-Feta Egg Scramble with Sweet Potato Hash over Rice** *(~15 min — busy night)*
   - **Main ingredients:** spinach, feta, eggs, sweet potato, rice.
   - **Fridge used:** spinach, feta, eggs, sweet potato, rice.
   - Quick scramble: sauté spinach + diced sweet potato, add beaten eggs, crumble feta.

3. **Wednesday — Chickpea, Egg & Spinach Skillet with Feta** *(~20 min)*
   - **Main ingredients:** chickpeas, spinach, eggs, feta, rice.
   - **Fridge used:** chickpeas, spinach, feta, eggs, rice.

4. **Thursday — Quick Egg Fried Rice with Chickpeas & Spinach, topped with Feta** *(~15 min — busy night)*
   - **Main ingredients:** rice, eggs, chickpeas, spinach, feta, soy sauce/greens (from list).
   - **Fridge used:** rice, eggs, chickpeas, spinach, feta.

5. **Friday — Spinach & Feta Egg Frittata with Rice** *(~20 min)*
   - **Main ingredients:** eggs, spinach, feta, rice.
   - **Fridge used:** eggs, spinach, feta, rice.

### Fridge Usage Notes
- **Spinach** (use soon) goes into nights 1–4 early — quick-cooking, so it fits busy nights.
- **Sweet potatoes** used nights 1 & 2 (roast/pan-cube quickly).
- **Feta** used across nights 2–5 — half a block stretches with eggs/chickpeas.
- **Chickpeas & rice** reused across meals so cans/rice portions aren’t wasted.

### Shopping List (grouped by store section)
*Only new items needed — leaving out what you already have (spinach, feta, eggs, sweet potatoes, rice, chickpeas).*

- **Produce:** onion, garlic, fresh ginger, fresh chili (optional), cilantro (optional)
- **Canned & Jarred:** coconut milk, curry paste, canned tomatoes (if needed for curry)
- **Pantry:** vegetable oil, salt, black pepper, soy sauce/tamari (for fried rice)
- **Dairy:** none needed this week

Let me know if you want full recipes or a different number of dinners!
