# Review Reply Drafts

Use this when someone has new customer reviews (Google, Yelp, app store, Etsy) and wants replies ready to paste.

## Inputs
- The review text, star rating, and reviewer first name for each review. Paste as many as you like.
- The business name and who signs the replies (from memory if saved).

## Steps
1. Sort the reviews: 1-2 stars first, then 3, then 4-5.
2. For each review, pull out the one concrete thing the reviewer mentioned (a dish, a delay, a staff member, a product flaw).
3. Write a reply of 30 to 70 words that names that concrete thing.
   - 4-5 stars: thank them, mention the detail, invite them back. No discount offers.
   - 3 stars: thank them, acknowledge what fell short, say what you will look at.
   - 1-2 stars: apologise once, no excuses, give the contact route from memory so it moves offline.
4. Flag any review that mentions safety, illness, a legal threat, or a staff member by full name as "Owner should read this first" and still draft a reply.

## Rules
- Never post, submit, or send a reply. Return text only.
- Never admit fault for safety or legal claims; say you want to talk directly.
- Do not promise refunds or freebies unless memory says you offer them.
- No copy-paste openers: each reply must start differently.

## Done when
Every review has a draft reply, and flagged reviews are listed at the top.
